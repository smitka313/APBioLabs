Help improve the Lab Bench

Found a bug or have an idea that would make these labs more useful for your students? Feedback from teachers is welcome.

Send a report

Use the GitHub issue form to report a problem or suggest an improvement. Include the lab number, device/browser, what you expected, and what happened. If a simulation failed, describe the steps to reproduce it.

Privacy: Do not include student names, student reports, Dataset IDs, screenshots containing student information, or other personally identifying data.

No GitHub account? Email instead

Send your feedback to rsmitka@roseville.k12.mi.us. Include the lab number, your device and browser, what you expected, and what happened. If the link doesn’t open an email app, copy the address into your school email. Please leave out student names and other identifying information.
