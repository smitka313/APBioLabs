Help improve the Lab Bench

Found a bug or have an idea that would make these labs more useful for your students? Feedback from teachers is welcome.

Send a report

Email feedback about the Lab Bench. Include the lab number, device/browser, what you expected, and what happened. If a simulation failed, describe the steps to reproduce it.

Privacy: Do not include student names, student reports, Dataset IDs, screenshots containing student information, or other personally identifying data.

Prefer GitHub?

You can also open a GitHub issue if you have an account. Email is an option for teachers who do not.
