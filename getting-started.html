Get started in five minutes

AP Bio Digital Lab Bench offers 13 free interactive investigations. Students work in their browser, collect simulated data, graph results, and export reports. No student account or installation is required.

Before class

  1. Choose a lab. Start with Photosynthesis, or choose any investigation from the lab list.
  2. Try the student workflow. Enter a test identity, answer a pre-lab question, run a trial, and inspect the data and graph.
  3. Share the direct lab URL. Post it in your LMS along with your instructions and due date.
  4. Decide what to collect. Ask students to submit their Word report (.docx) for the class dashboard, or a PDF if you only need to read and grade their work.
  5. Test on a school device. Check the site and report downloads using your school's browser and network.

Tell students

Open the assigned lab and enter your name and class period. Complete the sections your teacher assigns. Your work saves in this browser, not to a school account. Before switching computers or clearing browser data, download a progress file from Submit. When finished, export the report format your teacher requested.

After class

Open the class dashboard and add students' .docx reports or progress files. The dashboard processes them in your browser. It can show completion, key results, and pre-lab questions to revisit. A PDF cannot be imported as dashboard data.
