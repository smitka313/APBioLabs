AP Biology · Teacher quick start

Start teaching with the Lab Bench in five minutes

All 13 investigations are free, browser-based adaptations of the AP Biology investigative lab manual. No student accounts, installation or paid service is needed.

1. Choose a lab

Open the lab directory, select a lab, and try the Overview, Background and Pre-lab sections. Students can use phones, tablets and computers, although larger screens make graphs easier to read.

2. Share the link

Post the lab's URL in your LMS. Tell students which sections to complete and when the report is due. Projector mode can show the assigned sections and a timer.

3. Explain saving

Students enter their name and class period. Their answers save in that browser, not in a school account. On shared devices, save a progress file or transfer code before signing out. Moving to another device requires loading that backup; Word and PDF reports do not restore editable work.

4. Collect reports

In Submit, students download a Word report or save a PDF, check it, and upload it to your LMS. Downloading does not turn work in automatically. The report includes their Dataset ID and recorded work.

5. Review the class

Open the teacher dashboard and import downloaded student Word reports or progress files locally to review progress and export a CSV. It only shows files you import; it is not a live class roster or submission tracker.

Before assigning

Try one lab on the same type of school device students use. Check that JavaScript, downloads, pop-ups/print view, and the LMS upload workflow are allowed. Read the full teacher guide for troubleshooting and privacy details.
