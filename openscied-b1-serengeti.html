      How to play

      Share one device. One person or team is wildebeest, one is hyenas, and one is lions. There is no overall winner.

      1. Set up the board in winter. At the start of spring, summer and fall, the surviving wildebeest migrate. Each token normally touches another along a side.
      2. Wildebeest, hyenas and lions each draw a card in that order. Read and apply the result, even if it affects another agent.
      3. Hyenas hunt, then lions hunt where eligible. Sort the dice high to low; matching dice compete. The lower die is removed, ties and unmatched dice remain. Reroll only surviving dice until one side has none.
      4. Record what changes for all roles. Try the quick concept check that appears each season. After fall, compare your evidence across seasons.

      What this game models: Use four seasons of migration, event cards and dice-based hunts to explore short-term population changes. The game does not model wildebeest births, resource supply, prey-dependent changes in predator populations, or carrying capacity.
