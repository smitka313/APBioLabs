How to play

Start as carbon dioxide in the atmosphere. Roll once, follow the printed card and record. Staying put counts as a turn. Make at least 10 rolls; your teacher decides when to stop. Rolls do not represent equal lengths of time.

What this activity models · sources and limits

Based on OpenSciEd B.2 Lesson 9, Teacher Edition pp. 188 and 194–196, and the student Carbon/Energy Quantitative Model handout. All carbon starts in atmospheric carbon dioxide. Seven locations, a six-sided die, and at least ten recorded rolls follow the handout. Matter and energy categories use the handout’s summary table; “Biomass” matches that table (called “body mass” in its instructions).

This development version records choices from the printed direction cards. It does not validate those choices or supply unverified movement probabilities. Counts describe this journey; they are not measured global carbon stocks, residence times or energy quantities. Carbon atoms are conserved; energy transfers between systems and can leave as heat.

All station artwork is original procedural SVG created for this activity. It illustrates example systems, not quantitative inventories. Work is saved in this browser; downloading creates a local copy.
