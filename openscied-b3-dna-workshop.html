    What this model shows and what it leaves out

    This workshop uses two fictional examples, not actual genes or an entire cell. The molecule shapes are simplified diagrams, not exact structures. The labeled ends and arrows show strand order. Matching tiles stand in for chemical interactions. Real cells use enzymes, and copying a fragment here represents a small part of copying DNA before a cell divides.

    We model RNA made from one DNA template, then a ribosome reading a separate, longer practice message in groups of three. We omit RNA processing (such as adding a cap and tail or removing introns), the transfer molecules that bring amino acids, and the folding of a finished chain. A stop instruction does not add an amino acid.
