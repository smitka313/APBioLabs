What is LFS?

Li-Fraumeni syndrome (LFS) is inherited. A person is born with one p53 allele that does not fix mutations well. That raises the chance of several kinds of cancer. Not everyone with it gets cancer.

Words to know
Allele
One version of a gene.
Genotype
The alleles a person has.
Phenotype
A trait you can observe.
Homozygous
Two copies of the same allele.
Heterozygous
Two different alleles.
Gamete
An egg or sperm cell.

How alleles get passed on

Text description: a parent with chromosomes A-C and B-D. Without crossing over it makes gametes A-C and B-D. With crossing over it can also make A-D and B-C.

Your gametes

Sex cells

Eggs and sperm have 23 chromosomes, half of a body cell’s 46. The DNA is copied first.

Crossing over

Matching chromosomes line up and can swap sections. That makes new allele combinations.

Show more

The swap happens between the two chromosomes of a pair, one from each of that person’s parents.

Four gametes

Two divisions make four cells. When two gametes join, the new cell has 46 chromosomes.

      Part B · Lesson 9 · 13 · Reading

      How UV changes DNA, and how melanin helps

      UV hits DNA

      UV can change a DNA letter. A common change turns C into T.

      Show more

      This is a simplified picture. Real UV damage links neighboring letters, and copying then often puts in a T.

      Changed letter, changed protein

      A new letter can change an amino acid and the protein’s shape. A changed p53 may repair less well.

      Melanin is an umbrella

      Skin cells make melanin, a pigment. It absorbs UV and gives off heat, so less reaches the DNA. People differ in how much they make.

      Show more

      Dr. Jewel Plummer Cobb studied melanin and skin cancer, and did early research on cancer medicines.

      Try a C to T change

      Choose a group and a letter.

      Added by this site: four accuracy notes
      • Melanin is a pigment, not a protein.
      • The C-to-T picture simplifies UV damage.
      • Yeast repair genes are an analogy to p53, not p53.
      • LFS is an allele that does not fix mutations well, not a missing gene.
