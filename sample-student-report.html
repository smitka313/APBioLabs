Does a cover slow water loss?

Question and prediction

Would a cover reduce water loss from a shallow tray? I predicted that the covered trays would lose less water because the cover limits the exchange of air above the water.

Recorded results

I compared three covered trays with three uncovered trays. The trays started with the same amount of water and were in the same room for two hours. I measured the change in mass of each tray and its contents, with the cover in place for the covered group.

Evidence and explanation

The covered trays lost less water in every pair. They lost an average of 1.1 g, compared with 4.3 g for uncovered trays. The cover traps moist air near the water, which slows net evaporation. I measured mass, not humidity, so this is an explanation for the pattern rather than a direct measurement of what happened under the cover.

What to look for when grading

The explanation cites measured values with units and distinguishes the evidence from the proposed cause. Use your activity’s rubric to score those features. The completion mark does not do that for you.
