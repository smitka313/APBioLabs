Class dashboard

Drop your students’ lab reports (.docx) and progress files (.json) here to see who has finished, each student’s key results, the pre-lab questions to re-teach, and reports that may have been copied.

Nothing is uploaded. Your files are opened and read inside this browser window. They are not sent to any server, and nothing is saved after you close or reload the page. Once this page has loaded, it also works without an internet connection.

Add student files

Only reports made with this site carry the data the dashboard reads. A report that was printed to PDF, retyped or re-created in another program has no data in it.
